The Community panel used to be about everyone else — a feed to explore, posts to
react to. The new **You tab** makes it equally about you: your profile, your
boards, your conversations and your reputation, all in one place above your own
feed.

## Your profile, edited in place

The You tab opens with a hero: avatar, bio, follower and following counts, and
your posts underneath. Editing happens **inline** — change your bio right where
it's displayed, no settings detour. As you scroll your feed, the hero collapses
out of the way; it's a header, not a billboard.

Your posts are yours to manage here too — including deleting the ones that
didn't age well.

## Boards: curate what you save

**Boards** are moodboards for the community feed. See a widget post worth
keeping? **Save to board** sits right on the reaction bar. Boards you make
public appear on your profile, so your taste becomes something others can
browse — not just your output.

## An answer inbox, so replies stop getting lost

When your posts spark conversations, the responses now land in an **answer
inbox** on the You tab. Instead of re-scrolling threads to find who replied to
what, you get one place with every answer waiting for you.

## Reputation: tiers, rare medals, legendary bars

Contribution now earns **reputation**, GitHub-achievements style:

- **Achievements** track a stat each, with **bronze, silver and gold** tiers
- **Rare medals** are one-shot bars past gold
- **Legendary medals** sit above those — no tiers, just an outrageous threshold
- Every tier and medal adds **points**, and points bucket into **levels**

A reputation strip on the You tab shows where you stand, and a toast fires the
moment a new tier unlocks. Your reputation follows you around the app — account
switcher cards show points and social stats, so every profile carries its
record.

## Built to be shared

Posts and profiles picked up **cross-platform share menus**, and the feed got
livelier about telling you what's happening: toasts for new likes, comments,
followers and marketplace installs as they arrive.
