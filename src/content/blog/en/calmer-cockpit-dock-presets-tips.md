Not every release is a headline feature. This one is a dozen small decisions
about the pixels between you and your data — chrome that steps back when
you're working and steps in when you're lost.

## The dock became polite

Leave the workspace alone for a moment and the dock **folds itself into a
small puck**, melting onto the Nexow mark instead of sitting across your
canvas. Touch it and it wakes. Want it gone entirely? Hide it, or minimize
panels straight from the dock's context menu.

It's a small thing with a large effect: a full-screen chart is actually
full-screen now, and the controls are still one flick away.

## One click, an arranged canvas

The floating toolbar — which now starts mid-right, where your cursor actually
lives — picked up **auto-layout presets**. One click arranges the widgets on a
screen into a clean mosaic instead of a hand-nudged approximation of one.
Build first, tidy later, instantly.

Removing a widget now asks before it deletes — the one dialog nobody minds,
because free-form canvases deserve an undo moment for their most destructive
click.

## Tips that arrive in passing

Nexow has grown a lot of depth — bots with database sinks, agents with tool
grants, marketplace lineage — and nobody reads manuals. So the workspace now
teaches itself: occasional **tip toasts**, marked with a TIP badge, surface
one capability at a time, on a randomized cadence that stays out of your way.
Developer-flavored tips cover widgets, bots and agents; even context menus
chip in with pointers to what's behind them.

Each tip is a thing you can do immediately, not a tour you have to sit
through.

## Polish you'll feel, not see

The rest is texture: an accent beam that runs the chrome and carries across
panels, menus that reliably sit above modals, panels that stack correctly
above the accent line, and a splash screen that holds until the workspace has
actually painted — so Nexow opens *finished*, never half-dressed.

None of this adds a feature. All of it is why the features feel good.

[Launch Nexow](https://x.nexow.ai) and leave the mouse alone for a moment —
watch the cockpit put itself away.
