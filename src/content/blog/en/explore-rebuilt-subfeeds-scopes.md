Explore used to be a wall you scrolled. The rebuilt version is a space you
move through — every tile opens deeper instead of throwing you somewhere
else, and the feed finally answers the question every feed should: *whose
stuff am I looking at, and what is it built on?*

## Tiles that open, not navigate

Tap an Explore tile and it unfolds into an **endless sub-feed** right there in
the panel — related posts streaming under the one you picked, with a modal
view for reading a single post closely. Back out and you're exactly where you
left the grid. Even a refresh keeps your place: browsing state lives in the
URL, so where you were is where you return.

The grid itself is yours to shape now — **resize tiles** to give the good
stuff room, with one reaction bar that works the same everywhere.

## A feed scoped to your people

The new **scope toggle** flips Explore between the whole network and just
**your contacts** — the people you actually follow and talk to. Global for
discovery, contacts for signal. One tap, entirely your choice.

Curious about an author? Their profile opens as an **in-panel peek** — a card
over the feed, not a trip to another page. Full profiles, with their public
boards, are one more click when you want the whole picture.

## Widget posts show their ingredients

Every shared widget now declares **which connectors it uses**, stamped at
publish time — not guessed later. Before you install anything from the feed,
you can see it needs, say, Binance candles and nothing else. Publishing runs
through the same gate in reverse: you review your post's discovery metadata —
title, summary, sources — before it ships, so the feed stays searchable and
honest.

Behind Explore's chrome, one more tab appeared: a **governance teaser**. The
community deciding what Nexow builds next is on the roadmap — the tab marks
the spot.

[Launch Nexow](https://x.nexow.ai), open Community, and pull on a tile — it
goes deeper than it looks.
