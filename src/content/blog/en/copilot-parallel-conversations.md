Copilot was the second promise on the roadmap: an assistant that understands
your canvas, not just your prompt. It's now live in Nexow — and it arrived with
a trick we didn't originally promise: it can hold **several conversations at
once**, and they all keep working while you look away.

## A tab strip for your conversations

Copilot conversations now live in **tabs**, like a browser. Start a thread about
your ETH screen, open another to build a funding-rate widget, keep a third going
about layout — and switch between them freely. Each tab keeps its own context,
so you never have to re-explain what "this chart" means.

## Builds keep running in the background

The important part: a conversation doesn't stop when you leave it. Ask Copilot
to build a widget, switch tabs, rearrange your canvas, go do something else —
the build **keeps running in the background** and the tab lights up when it's
done.

Builds are **durable**, too. If a run gets interrupted — network blip, closed
laptop, an errant refresh — Copilot picks the build back up instead of losing
it. You come back to a finished widget, not a half-written one.

## It asks before it guesses

A prompt like *"make me a volatility widget"* leaves real decisions open: which
market? what window? chart or number? Copilot now **asks clarifying questions**
when your request is ambiguous, then builds once it actually knows what you
want. Fewer plausible-but-wrong widgets, more first-try keepers.

## It can work the canvas itself

Copilot has **layout tools**: it can place what it builds onto your free-form
canvas, organize screens, and arrange groups — so "build it and put it next to
the order book" is one request, not a build followed by manual housekeeping.

## Part of the workspace, not a separate app

Copilot lives in the same panel system as everything else, next to your widgets,
bots and agents. It reads the context you give it and acts on the workspace you
already have — a second pair of hands, not a second application.

[Launch Nexow](https://x.nexow.ai), open Copilot, and start two conversations at
once. That's the moment it clicks.
