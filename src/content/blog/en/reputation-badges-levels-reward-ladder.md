Nexow has quietly been counting. Every widget you build, every bot you run,
every post that lands and every asset someone installs feeds a single number on
your profile — **reputation points**. Points unlock badges, badges unlock
levels, and when the Nexow DAO opens, both start paying out in credits and
governance tokens.

Here is the whole system, thresholds and all.

## Points are just your work, counted

Reputation isn't a mystery ranking. Nexow computes it from your own activity,
**on your device**, with no server scoring you against anybody else. Each action
carries a fixed weight:

- **25 pts** — publish an asset to the marketplace
- **15 pts** — create and run a bot
- **10 pts** — an install of your published work, a new board, a moderation
  report filed
- **5 pts** — build a widget, publish a post, open a DM thread
- **3 pts** — gain a follower
- **2 pts** — add a mutual contact, receive a like
- **1 pt** — write or receive a comment, give a like or reaction, follow someone

The weights say what Nexow actually values: **publishing beats posting, and
building beats both**. Twenty-five points for shipping something other people
can install; one point for a like.

## The badge vault: 34 medals, three rarities

On top of raw activity sit **34 badges**, each tracking exactly one stat.

**18 tiered achievements** come in bronze, silver and gold — 54 tiers in total.
*Storyteller* wants 1, 10 then 50 posts. *Architect* wants 1, 10 then 50
widgets. *Automator* wants 1, 3 then 10 bots. *Night owl* only counts posts
published between 00:00 and 05:00 local time.

**10 rare medals** are single hard bars set past gold, with no partial credit —
*Oracle* at 500 posts, *Foundry* at 100 published assets, *Benefactor* at 10,000
likes given.

**6 legendary medals** ask for something absurd: *Overmind* at 50 bots, *Grand
architect* at 250 widgets, *Immortal* at 1,000 days on Nexow.

Every unlock adds a flat bonus on top of the activity that earned it — **+20**
per tier, **+100** per rare medal, **+250** per legendary. Clear the entire
catalogue and the bonuses alone are worth **3,580 points**.

## Five levels, one bar

Points bucket into levels, and the medallion in your account header changes with
each one:

- **Bronze** — from 0 points
- **Silver** — from 250
- **Gold** — from 1,000
- **Platinum** — from 5,000
- **Legend** — from 20,000

Points, badges and levels are **live in the app today**. Open your profile and
the strip is already populated with whatever you've built so far.

## The reward ladder — planned, and published up front

Here's the part that ships with the DAO. Each unlock is scheduled to pay twice:
in **credits**, the spendable AI generation you use to build widgets, and in
**NXW**, the Solana governance token of the Nexow DAO.

Badge unlocks, per badge:

- **Bronze tier** — 500 credits + 10 NXW, on each of 18 badges
- **Silver tier** — 1,500 credits + 25 NXW, on each of 18 badges
- **Gold tier** — 5,000 credits + 90 NXW, on each of 18 badges
- **Rare medal** — 25,000 credits + 250 NXW, on each of 10 medals
- **Legendary medal** — 100,000 credits + 1,000 NXW, on each of 6 medals

Level-ups, claimed once each:

- **Reach Silver** — 4,000 credits + 250 NXW
- **Reach Gold** — 20,000 credits + 1,000 NXW
- **Reach Platinum** — 100,000 credits + 3,000 NXW
- **Reach Legend** — 500,000 credits + 10,000 NXW

Claim every tier, every medal and every level and the complete set comes to
**1,600,000 credits and 25,000 NXW**. At the list rate of $87 per million
credits, that's roughly **$139 of AI generation** — about two months of
Sponsor-grade building, earned rather than billed.

Same schedule for everyone, on every plan, **including Free**.

We're publishing the numbers before they're live on purpose. Points, badges and
levels work today; the credit and NXW payouts arrive with the DAO, and this
schedule is the proposal going to the first ballot. Every surface that shows it
carries a *planned* chip until it does.

## Start with the easy ones

The bronze tiers are deliberately gentle — one post, one widget, one bot, one
board. Most of them fall out of a single afternoon of building. The gold tiers
are a season's work, and the legendary six are a running joke that somebody will
eventually ruin by actually earning them.
