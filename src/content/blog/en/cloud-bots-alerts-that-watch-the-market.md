A dashboard is great when you're watching it. The problem is you can't watch it
all day. That's what **bots** are for — cloud automations that keep an eye on your
data and fire signals to your widgets the moment something happens, whether your
tab is open or not.

Bots are live in Nexow today. Here's how they work.

## Describe an alert in plain words

The fastest way to make a bot is to say what you want to know:

> Tell me when BTC drops 5% in an hour.

Nexow turns that sentence into a running automation — it watches the feed,
evaluates the condition on a schedule, and pings you when it triggers. No cron
jobs, no glue code, no server to keep alive.

## Or wire it visually

Prefer to build the logic by hand? Bots also compose from visual building blocks:

- **Thresholds** — price above or below a level
- **% change** — moves over a rolling window
- **Moving-average crossovers** — a fast MA crossing a slow one
- **Digests** — a scheduled summary instead of a live trip

Mix them into exactly the rule you want, and adjust it whenever your thesis
changes.

## They run in the cloud, tab closed

The important part: bots don't run in your browser. They run on a **cloud
heartbeat**, so they keep checking your conditions after you've closed the laptop.
Come back to a signal that fired at 3am, not a chart you missed.

## Signals land on your canvas

When a bot triggers, the signal flows straight back to your widgets — the same
canvas where you build everything else. Your alerts live next to the charts that
generated them, not buried in a separate inbox.

## Bots today, agents next

Bots are the first step in putting your workspace on autopilot. Next come
**agents** — AI with a full harness of model, memory, skills, tools and triggers
that can watch, reason and act on their own — and a workspace **Copilot**. Bots
are live now; the rest is landing soon.
