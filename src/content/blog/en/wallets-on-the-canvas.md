Exchange data tells you what the market is doing. Your wallet tells you what
*you* are holding. Nexow's connector catalog now covers both: **MetaMask,
Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet and Ledger** all connect directly, and
everything downstream — widgets, bots, AI codegen — knows what to do with them.

## Five wallets, many chains

Connect a browser wallet with a click, or a **Ledger** for keys that never touch
a browser extension. Tracking is **multi-chain**: one connection follows your
addresses across networks instead of treating each chain as a separate account.

The small frictions are handled too. No wallet installed? Nexow points you at
the right install page instead of failing silently. Switched accounts inside
your wallet? Nexow notices and offers to track the new address alongside the
old one.

Connections stay read-oriented: Nexow watches balances and activity. Your keys
sign nothing.

## Bots that watch your wallet

The bot engine gained two wallet processors, and they work like every other bot
— describe what you want in a sentence, or wire it visually:

- **Wallet activity** — fires when new transactions hit a watched address
- **Wallet balance** — tracks your balance's value and fires on meaningful
  moves, with the baseline and percentage change in the signal

They run on the same cloud heartbeat as every bot, tab closed. Wake up to *"your
wallet moved 4.2% overnight"* instead of opening an explorer to check.

## Prompt widgets from your own addresses

AI codegen now understands wallet data sources. Ask for *"a widget showing my
wallet's balance history"* and the generated code is fed by your connected
addresses — the same way it already handles exchange candles and trades. Wallet
widgets, wallet bots and wallet connections compose like everything else on the
free-form canvas.

## The catalog keeps growing

Wallets aren't the only new connectors. The catalog picked up **open-data
services** and **browser, scraping and search services** like Browserbase — plus
quick filters to cut the growing list down to what's public or ready to use
right now.

[Launch Nexow](https://x.nexow.ai), open Connections, and put your wallet next
to your charts — then set a bot to watch it.
