The Nexow preview already does a lot: describe a widget and it writes the code,
runs it sandboxed, and streams live data from 28+ connectors onto a free-form
canvas — with cloud bots watching your data and free accounts syncing it across
devices. That's what shipped. Here's what's next.

## Agents with a real harness

Bots watch and alert. **Agents** go further: they're AI with a full harness —
**model, memory, skills, tools, knowledge and triggers** — running in the cloud.
You give an agent a job and it watches, reasons and acts on its own, on a
schedule or in response to an event.

Think of a bot as a tripwire and an agent as a teammate: one tells you BTC dropped
5%, the other can be told to *investigate why, check the funding rate and the
order book, and summarize what it found.*

## A Copilot for your whole workspace

**Copilot** is an assistant that understands your canvas. Attach a widget's
screenshot or its live data and ask:

> Explain this chart. What should I watch next?

Copilot reads the context you give it and answers in plain language — a second set
of eyes on the dashboard you already built, without leaving it.

## A marketplace and strategy signals

The network gets an economy. In the **marketplace**, publish widgets others can
drop straight onto their canvas, and pick up ready-made ones for yours. With
**strategy signals**, publish an audited track record, let others subscribe to the
signals you trust, and earn on the strategies you run.

## The principle stays fixed

New surfaces, same rule: **private by default.** Local mode — bring-your-own-key
codegen, browser persistence, direct-to-venue connections — isn't going away as we
add hosted agents and server components. The cloud is always something you opt
into, never the default.

Under the hood, generation runs on Anthropic's Claude — **Opus 4.8** by default,
with **Sonnet 4.6** and **Haiku 4.5** for faster, cheaper builds — and in private
mode you supply your own key, so you stay in control of usage and cost.
